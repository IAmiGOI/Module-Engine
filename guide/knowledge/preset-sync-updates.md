These live on the settings screen: [Preset](stme:card:preset) exports and imports the engine's whole configuration; [Sync](stme:card:sync) keeps settings and chats in sync between devices (cloud or GitHub); [Updates](stme:card:updates) checks for and installs new versions of the extension; [Backgrounds](stme:card:backgrounds) manages chat backgrounds.
