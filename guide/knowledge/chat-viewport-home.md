[Chat Viewport](stme:card:chatViewport) (experimental) replaces SillyTavern's chat view with the engine's own: glyph panels that group consecutive messages, collapsed reasoning and tool calls, a generation status line, resizable chat width. With it on, the home screen becomes a **desktop** of blocks — recent chats, characters, quick actions and widgets (clock, characters, and this guide). Blocks can be dragged; "Add widget" adds more.
